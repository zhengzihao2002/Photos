## English

- Settings → Updates now shows the changes in every version newer than your installed version, newest first.
- Release notes stay visible while an update downloads. Missing notes do not prevent updating.
- Update descriptions support English and Simplified Chinese.

## 简体中文

- 设置 → 更新现在会按从新到旧的顺序，显示当前已安装版本之后各个版本的更新内容。
- 下载更新时仍可查看更新说明。说明缺失或加载失败不会阻止更新。
- 更新说明支持英文和简体中文。
